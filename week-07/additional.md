## Extension — Additional Interpreters

The original CAR-2014-04-003 analytic is focused on PowerShell.

We expanded our threat hunting project by applying similar
process execution investigation principles to additional
interpreters.

The added interpreters are Python, JavaScript, and Windows CMD.

These additions are custom detection queries, not official
MITRE CAR-2014-04-003 detection rules.

All activities are mapped to the appropriate MITRE ATT&CK
T1059 sub-techniques.
